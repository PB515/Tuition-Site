## 3. What the Best Ones Do Right
- **Lead with the teacher as a person** (Pi Square = "Dharmesh Sir"). Parents buy a teacher.
- **Proof-forward** — result screenshots, Google reviews.
- **Instant contact** — WhatsApp/call front-and-centre.
- **Seasonal exam hooks** (Vyas GUJCET crash tied to exam date).
- **Own the directories** — the visible players are on Justdial/Sulekha/UrbanPro/Google.

## 4. What to Avoid
- **No website at all** → invisible beyond the Instagram algorithm; can't rank or be vetted.
- **Over-claiming** ("guaranteed 90%+") → erodes trust, legally shaky.
- **Generalist blur** → "we teach everything 6–12" dilutes a maths-specialist message.
- **Directory-only dependence** → price-competing beside 2,000 tutors, no brand.
- **Thin templated pages** → doorway-page trap.

## 5. THE GAP (the killer-feature space)
**No one in Vadodara is a maths-only specialist with (a) a real conversion website, (b) self-serve interactivity, and (c) a structured parent-communication system.** Maths specialists are invisible (Instagram-only); web-present players are generalist science institutes. Parents choose via directories, reels and word-of-mouth — with **no way to self-diagnose the child's actual problem.**

**Killer feature:** a free **Maths Concept-Gap Test** — first question routes by class, 8–10 chapter-tagged questions return a personalised weakness read, result captures a qualified lead and ends in a **WhatsApp admission enquiry** ("Sir will target these chapters"). No competitor has anything like it.

## 6. Recommended Features (phased)

**MVP / Phase 1 (launch — convert enquiries):** segment-router homepage · **Concept-Gap Test** · About Snehal Soni Sir (teacher-as-hero) · 5 differentiated SEO pages · Results (real proof) · Teaching Method · Contact + Map · short enquiry form (consent + honeypot) · WhatsApp/call CTAs · **Google Business Profile + reviews** (highest-ROI local-SEO lever). *Complexity: M (the test is the one non-trivial build).*

**Phase 1.5 — Lead dashboard:** admin login; enquiry status New→Enquired→Visited→Joined; WhatsApp follow-up. *(Auth app → PART 7.) Complexity: M.*

**Phase 2 — Student management:** students/parents DB, batches, attendance, weekly marks, exam dates, fees; **one-click prefilled `wa.me`, admin sends manually**. *Complexity: M–L.*

**Phase 3 — Parent/Student portal + PWA:** logins, per-child private data. *⚠️ Highest security bar — cross-user denial gate non-negotiable. WhatsApp Business API automation = BSP + Meta templates + per-message cost. Complexity: L.*

## 7. Strategic Summary
Win by being **the** maths-specialist brand Vadodara can't currently find online: a credible named teacher (Snehal Soni Sir, 25+ yrs), a real conversion site, and a free Concept-Gap Test that turns an anxious parent's vague worry into a specific diagnosis and a WhatsApp enquiry — backed by real results (Navrachana 97/100) and a structured parent-update system rivals only do ad-hoc. Don't price-fight 2,000 tutors in the directories; own the directories *and* Google Business Profile for distribution, but convert on a branded site and a tool no rival has. The maths specialists are invisible and the visible players aren't maths specialists — that exact gap is the whole opportunity.
