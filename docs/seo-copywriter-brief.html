SEO Page Copywriting Brief

How to write the custom copy for our 17 search pages so they rank well and sound like us. You don't touch any code — you write words into a simple template, we put them live.

Your job, in one line: for each page, write 2–3 short paragraphs and pick one image. That's it.

Everything else on the page already exists. The FAQs rarely change — only add one if it's genuinely useful.

What you provide

2–3 paragraphs of fresh copy, plus one photo (1200×900). The paragraphs sit beside the image, and the image side flips from page to page automatically, so no two pages look alike.

How long?

About 2 short paragraphs per page. Don't pad — short and clear beats long and padded. Google now rewards genuinely useful writing, not word count.

2 Voice & tone

Sound like this

  • Warm, plain, calm — an experienced teacher reassuring a worried parent.
  • Call him Snehal Sir or Sir.
  • Short sentences. Answer the real worry: “will my child get attention, will marks improve?”
  • Use “maths”, Indian/British spelling.

Avoid this

  • Hype words: “best-in-class”, “world-class”, “guaranteed”.
  • “The faculty / our team” — it is Sir himself.
  • Salesy, pushy or exam-coaching-factory tone.
  • Repeating the keyword over and over.

3 Three hard rules (please never break these)

① Only two branches

New Sama Road (main) & Alkapuri. For Karelibaug, Fatehgunj, etc. write “near / for students from”, never “in”. Never imply a branch that doesn't exist.

② One hard number

The only statistic we claim is 97 out of 100 in Class 12 Applied Maths. Invent no other numbers, counts or percentages.

③ Stay factual

OK to state: 25+ years, ex-Head of Maths at Navrachana, teaches 9–12 himself, batches max 20, weekly tests, NCERT (CBSE+GSEB), boards+JEE+GUJCET. Unsure? Leave it out.

4 The image

1200 × 900 px
(4:3)

One photo per page, 1200 × 900 px (4:3 shape). A real classroom / teaching photo is best; you can reuse one across similar pages if needed.

Upload it in Admin → Website → (the page) → Upload. Any JPG or PNG works and any file size — it is optimized automatically, quality kept. The placeholder disappears once a photo is added.
